## 1. Product Positioning & Value Proposition

### What problem does TaskFlow solve?
High-velocity engineering and product teams suffer from fragmentation: spreadsheets lack real-time status and auditability, while basic todo apps lack multi-project hierarchies, blocker tracking, dependency management, and enterprise RBAC. TaskFlow bridges this gap by unifying planning (Kanban, Calendar, Timeline), execution (subtasks, activity history, comments), and governance (immutable audit logs, health telemetry) into a single cohesive system.

### Target Persona
- **Engineering Leads & Engineering Managers**: Require visibility across project milestones, team member workloads, and delivery blockers without micro-management.
- **Full-Stack Developers**: Need frictionless task tracking, keyboard-driven navigation (`Cmd+K`), and contextual AI task decomposition.
- **Compliance & Operations Officers**: Require full SOC2/audit readiness with non-repudiable audit trails of task mutations and role modifications.

## 3. Product Cohesion & Feature Alignment

- **Are features disconnected?** No. Projects group tasks; tasks generate activity logs and notifications; audit logs monitor security-sensitive events; reports calculate real throughput metrics.
- **Product Vocabulary**: Consistent terminology used throughout:
  - `Workspace` -> `Projects` -> `Tasks` -> `Subtasks`
  - `Blocker` (Reason + Flag)
  - `Activity Log` (Field-level changes) vs `Audit Log` (Security/Administrative events)
